Right now: AI adoption at REMA 1000 / It is — in Norway

01Now

AI at REMA 1000, since January 2026

My main job this year has been building REMA 1000's AI capability: from the first solutions in production to broad adoption, responsible governance and benefits we can measure.

i.

Development

Architecture and development of AI solutions, agents and assistants connected to core systems and integrations. From prototype to production — secure, observable and maintainable.

ii.

Adoption

Making AI an everyday tool. Training, champions and use cases that meet real needs across the business — not just in IT.

iii.

Governance

A framework for responsible use: policies, risk assessment, data access and compliance with regulation such as the EU AI Act and GDPR — without slowing innovation down.

iv.

Benefits realisation

From potential to impact. Benefit maps, measurement and follow-up, so every AI investment can be explained in time saved, quality and business value.

The hard part of AI is rarely the model. It is getting it adopted, governed and measured.
